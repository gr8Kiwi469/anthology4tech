If you need to report an attribution correction, request removal of content, or ask a question about the Anthology project, you can reach the curator directly.

## Contact Options

### Email
**anthology.curator@anthologyatelier.com**  

### LinkedIn
If you prefer, you can message me through LinkedIn.

### What to Include
To help me respond quickly, please include:
- The article title or subject  
- The LinkedIn presenter’s name  
- The reason for your request  
- Any supporting links or screenshots  

I aim to respond within 48 hours.
