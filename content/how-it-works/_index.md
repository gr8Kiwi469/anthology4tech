Anthology Atelier and its sub‑sites curate publicly shared technology content from trusted sources such as LinkedIn. The goal is to preserve, organize, and present valuable insights in a clean, searchable, and continuously updated format.

## What We Curate
- Technology articles originally posted by their respective presenters  
- Visual content such as diagrams, slides, and animated images  
- Links to original posts for attribution and verification  

## What We Do *Not* Do
- We do not write or author the curated articles  
- We do not claim ownership or copyright  
- We do not modify the original content beyond formatting for readability  
- We do not include comments or follow‑on discussions

## Why This Exists
Professionals often lose track of valuable posts buried in feeds. Anthology solves this by:
- Providing a permanent, organized archive  
- Offering a subscriber‑only index of curated content  
- Allowing readers to explore topics without social‑media noise  

## Notes & Requests
- Anthology does not claim to include all available articles on any topic; we curate what we find
- Please report any duplicate material so we can adjust the archive accordingly
- Our vault currently contains 2,500+ articles collected over the past six months, increasing by 10–30 articles daily
- The initial launch will include 150–200 curated articles, with 150–200 added each month

## How Subscriptions Will Work
Subscribers will gain access to:
- The full curated article index  
- Monthly content drops  
- Topic‑based collections  
- Optional email notifications  

## Attribution & Rights
All rights remain with the original presenters.  
We provide curation only — not authorship.
